Teaching

Courses Taught

  • Large Language Models (DS 5983, EECE 5668) — A graduate course I developed from scratch at Northeastern University, focusing on the theory, training, and applications of large language models for tasks like text generation, summarization, and question answering (2024–2026).
  • Supervised Machine Learning (DS 5220, EECE 5644) — Covers foundational algorithms and theory in supervised learning including SVMs, decision trees, neural networks, and learning theory such as bias-variance and VC dimensions (2021–2026).
  • Software Engineering (EECE 4520) — An undergraduate course exploring structured and agile methodologies, UML, design patterns, estimation, and full lifecycle project development (2021–2026).
  • Introduction to Data Management and Processing (DS 5110) — Introduces students to relational databases and data processing tools used in data science, including SQL, normalization, and query optimization (2021–2024).
  • Unsupervised Machine Learning and Data Mining (DS 5230) — Focuses on clustering, dimensionality reduction, pattern mining, and unsupervised techniques for extracting insights from large-scale data (2020–2023).
  • Computational Methods for Data Analytics (EECE 2300) — Teaches Python programming and fundamental algorithms for data analytics, including regression, classification, and decomposition (2020–2021).
  • Artificial Intelligence (CS 4100) — An undergraduate AI course covering search, planning, uncertainty, ML (supervised and reinforcement), and deep learning (Spring 2020).
  • Data Science Bootcamp — A 6-month intensive bootcamp for training graduate students in data science, including hands-on coding, projects, and exams (Experis Academy, 2018).
  • Advanced Programming — A Bar-Ilan University course covering advanced topics like multithreading, sockets, memory, GUI design, and patterns (2016–2017).
  • Multi-Robot Systems — An advanced robotics course on distributed algorithms and coordination architectures for multi-robot teams (Bar-Ilan University, 2014–2016).
  • Introduction to Robotics — A Bar-Ilan undergraduate course; as a TA, I developed a full ROS-based tutorial and lab suite adopted globally (2013–2017).
  • Computer Science Courses — Taught numerous undergrad CS courses at the College of Management Academic Studies, including Data Structures, OS, DBs, Cloud Computing, and more (2013–2017).
